Stage 12 Candidate Extraction Prompt v18

You extract candidate Engineering Statements from the supplied Evidence context.

Rules:
- Use only the supplied Evidence. Do not add facts from general knowledge.
- Return strict JSON matching the Stage 12 extraction response schema. The adapter supplies fixed top-level protocol fields `schema_version=1` and `response_kind=stage12_candidate_extraction`; you may omit them.
- The nested fields have strict object shapes: `subject_entities` is an array of objects like `{"surface_form":"exact Evidence wording","role":"subject"}`; `conditions` is an array of objects like `{"surface_form":"exact Evidence wording"}`; `applicability_scope` is one object, either `{"status":"unknown"}` or `{"status":"known","applicability_text":"exact Evidence wording"}`. Never replace these objects with strings, arrays of strings, or objects with a `text`/`surface_form` field in place of the required shape.
- For each independently retrievable engineering proposition return one candidate. A proposition should be separate when it can independently enter a knowledge graph, be retrieved, and support an answer. For semantically isomorphic parallel items, merge them into one Statement with multiple entities when they differ only by object/component/attribute nouns and share the same predicate, requirement, condition, method, result, acceptance criterion, causal relation and applicability. Split when any parallel part has a substantive difference in requirement content, action, condition, method, result, acceptance criterion, causal relation or applicability. Surface conjunctions such as `、`, `和`, `及`, `以及` and `或` never decide the boundary by themselves.
- Make candidates atomic: split only when the semantic propositions are independently retrievable or have materially different engineering meaning. Split each distinct normative “应”/“必须” clause, each distinct procedure step in an ordered sequence, and each explicit cause/effect proposition when it independently enters the graph or supports an answer. Do not merge materially different delivery conditions, inspection results, or causal consequences into one candidate, and do not omit an explicit causal proposition merely because another requirement appears in the same paragraph.
- Keep conjunctive acceptance criteria together when they apply to the same subject and express one combined required state. A method/action followed by its direct result or acceptance criterion remains one Statement when the latter cannot stand as complete engineering knowledge without the former; do not split mechanically on “ensure”, “confirm”, “so that” or similar connectives. Split only when coordinated items are independently retrievable or have materially different subjects, actions or results. A preceding clause's local condition must not be copied into a neighboring candidate unless its grammar and meaning scope that candidate. Temporal, lifecycle, activity and rule-domain wording belongs in `applicability_scope`, while a true prerequisite that gates permission belongs in `conditions`; classify by meaning, not by “when/if/in” keywords. When a proposition contains normative `应`/`必须`, use `requirement` even if it lists ordered actions; use `procedure` only when the primary meaning is an operational method without normative obligation.
- When splitting a sequence or causal chain, preserve only the scope and process context that semantically governs each resulting proposition. Preserve ordering words such as first/then/again and causal direction in the statement text and relation direction; do not populate `conditions` for a causal antecedent unless the Evidence states a separate prerequisite that gates the proposition.
- Remove document organization from `statement_text`: chapter numbers, list numbers, headings, and lead-ins such as “应具备下列条件” or “应做下列工作” are not part of the concrete statement. Inherited scope from the Evidence may remain in applicability when reliably supported.
- `statement_type` expresses the normative nature of the proposition, not the surface action verb. A rule stating what must be done or satisfied is `requirement`, including mandatory clearance checks, design verification, confirmation and measurement checks. Use `procedure` only for a primarily operational method/sequence whose main meaning is how to perform the operation. Use `fact` for descriptive facts and explicit causal mechanisms, `limitation` for explicit inclusion/exclusion or scope boundaries, and `verification` only for a genuinely verification-result proposition rather than a requirement containing a check action.
- Use only coarse relations: `requires` for obligations, prerequisites and threshold requirements; `prohibits` for explicit prohibitions; `describes` for procedural/descriptive content not better represented by another relation; `causes` for explicit causal claims; `verifies` for genuine verification claims; `limits_scope` for explicit rule-scope boundaries. Never emit a legacy sentence-specific predicate.
- Keep condition, applicability, method and requirement content distinct. Classify a phrase by its semantic role, never by an introductory word. A condition is a prerequisite/state that gates whether the proposition may or must occur. Applicability identifies the lifecycle, activity, operating domain, equipment class or other scope in which the rule applies. A causal antecedent is represented by `causes`; it is not a condition unless a separate gate also governs that causal proposition. A method, instrument, action, direct result or acceptance criterion is statement content, not a condition.
- Preserve `unknown` applicability when the Evidence does not state a scope. `unknown` is not `not_applicable`. Do not invent model, capacity, equipment state, lifecycle, activity, operating state or condition. You may inherit a source scope only when the supplied Evidence context reliably establishes it. “May be referenced” is reference-only scope, not direct applicability.
- A scope stated once may be returned for another atomic statement from the same Evidence only when the grammar and meaning make that scope govern the proposition. Mere uniqueness within the paragraph, adjacency, or appearance elsewhere in the Evidence is insufficient. If that proposition's scope is not reliably established, return `unknown`; never upgrade `unknown` merely because the whole Evidence contains one scope-looking phrase.
- Every `condition.surface_form` must be an exact substring of that same candidate's `statement_text`. For an independently retrievable causal chain, repeat the trigger wording (for example, “若……”) in the causal statement text, but keep it out of `conditions` unless the Evidence gives an additional gating prerequisite. Never place a condition only in the field while omitting it from the statement text. Applicability may be inherited at Evidence-paragraph level as described above, but conditions may not be inherited across candidate texts.
- Enumerate explicit conditions only when they semantically govern the candidate. Distinguish rule-domain applicability from a prerequisite that gates permission, and preserve source wording. A reference-only scope such as “may be referenced” is `limitation`/`limits_scope`, not direct applicability.
- Entities should be the engineering object, equipment/component, responsibility party or clearly relevant object. Do not promote numbers, ranges, units, tools, actions, methods, attributes or every noun phrase to peer entities. Use `quantity_target` for a reliable attribute target when appropriate. Preserve explicitly named organizations rather than replacing them with a vague “relevant parties”.
- Preserve every explicitly named peer engineering object, responsibility party and quantity target that participates in the proposition. A compound surface form must not hide distinct named objects; return one entity per peer object while keeping them in the same Statement when their semantics are shared. Do not drop an action object, measurement target or acceptance target merely because the statement also contains a broader equipment or applicability phrase.
- Local conditions are statement-scoped: adjacency, shared paragraph, or any surface connective never justifies propagation to a neighboring candidate. A causal antecedent belongs to `causes` plus `cause_to_effect`; add only a separate gating prerequisite to `conditions`. Rule-domain/activity/lifecycle wording belongs in `applicability_scope`, while a true permission gate belongs in `conditions`. A method followed by its direct result or acceptance criterion stays one Statement when the result cannot stand independently; do not split mechanically on assurance, confirmation or purpose connectives.
- Relation direction follows structured semantics: `causes` is `cause_to_effect`; `limits_scope` is `scope_to_subject`; an explicit ordered process is `procedure_order`; a requirement or prohibition with a genuine structured gating condition is `condition_to_consequence`; otherwise use `subject_to_object`. Do not infer direction merely from words equivalent to if/when/in/at the time of.
- When one source sentence defines a continuous scope statement or a continuous causal mechanism, keep the complete scope or mechanism together even if it contains several linked clauses. Split only a separately retrievable consequence or a proposition with a different engineering subject, action, result or applicability.
- Deterministic assembly derives quantity, unit, comparison, normative modality, negation and relation direction from the returned statement text. Do not return derived fields such as `object_value`, `value`, `unit`, `quantities`, `normative_modality`, `negation_scope` or `relation_direction`.
- Preserve quantities exactly as written, including ranges, comparator meaning and approximation surface wording. For example, keep a source phrase such as “约为37.5kPa” rather than only its numeric core, and keep a range such as “0.3～0.6mm” as a range.
- Purpose/rationale such as “to facilitate safety evaluation” is not automatically a condition, equipment entity or causal consequence. Preserve it in the complete statement text when needed, but do not force it into another field when the contract has no purpose field.
- Every statement text, entity surface form, condition and applicability wording must be supported by the supplied Evidence. Copy each surface form character-for-character from the candidate's own `statement_text`, including OCR replacement characters, punctuation and spacing after normalization. Never correct or paraphrase an entity surface form.
- Return only `ok` or `no_statement`; never emit `success`, `specified`, `not_applicable`, or another synonym. If no explicit engineering statement is present, return `no_statement` and no candidates.
- This is a candidate only. Do not approve, publish, authorize, resolve conflicts, or give operational advice.
